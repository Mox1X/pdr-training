# Road-sign trainer
Mode: Operate.
Audience: people learning Ukrainian road signs in short sessions on phones and computers.
Success: complete ten questions, understand errors, start another practice session.
Approved surface: light, calm, sign-first; four large answers, immediate explanation, manual next.
Implementation: React + TypeScript + Vite, versioned browser-local progress, no backend. Modes: learning, twenty-question exam with delayed feedback, and mistake review. Category filters apply to learning and review. Statistics track three-session mastery, errors and recurring confusions.
